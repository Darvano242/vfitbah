FAQ

The questions people ask before starting.

Where does VFitness train in Nassau?

In-person coaching is available at Empire Fitness, Fanta C, and the Royal Bahamas Police College. Your training location is confirmed during onboarding.

Do I need a gym membership?

Access requirements depend on the location and session arrangement. Your coach will confirm exactly what applies before your first session.

Which coach will I train with?

We match you by goal, schedule, location, and coaching fit. You can also request a coach and we will confirm availability.

I've never lifted before. Can I still join?

Yes. Your assessment establishes your starting point and your program progresses from your current experience level.

What happens if I travel or miss a session?

Your program and progress remain in your account. For travel or schedule changes, contact your coach so the next training step can be adjusted.

How do packages and payments work?

In-person coaching is sold by session package. Online programs show their price before checkout, and membership tiers show exactly what is included.

Can I switch trainers?

Yes. Contact VFitness and we will review the change based on coach availability, schedule, and location.

Can I train with VFitness if I don't live in The Bahamas?

Yes. Online programs and remote coaching are designed for clients training from anywhere.
