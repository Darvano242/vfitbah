What you get

The parts of the job that take coaches away from coaching are already built.

  • Clients sent your way

    People who ask for a consultation on vfitbah.com are matched to a coach with room in their week.

  • Bookings and payments handled

    Packages, sessions and payments run through the site. You log who trained and the system keeps count.

  • The VFIT app for your clients

    Every package holder gets nutrition planning, weight tracking and online training between sessions.

  • A team and a path

    You work beside experienced coaches, with senior roles as your client list grows.

What we look for

  • A recognised personal training certification, or one in progress.
  • Current CPR and first aid.
  • Good with people. You can run a small group and still coach each person in it.
  • Reliable hours in Nassau, early mornings or evenings especially.

How pay works. You earn a share of every package your clients buy. We go through the numbers with you on the first call.
